# Elpis Property Limited — Operational Workflow

This guide describes the intended workflow for the Elpis Property platform. Business policies, branch assignments, commission rates and approval limits must be confirmed by Elpis management before production.

## User roles

The inherited application supports Managing Director, Branch Manager, Team Lead, Marketer, Finance and Client roles. Elpis must confirm the role assignments and permissions before launch.

## Core workflow

1. Management configures branches, staff roles and business settings.
2. Marketers register leads and follow up prospective clients.
3. Authorized staff create property listings and maintain property records.
4. Client onboarding captures required identity and next-of-kin details.
5. Finance staff review payment records and reconcile provider notifications.
6. Commission calculations and approvals follow Elpis-approved policies.
7. Management reviews branch performance, revenue and audit reports.
8. Client users access their own profile, property allocations, payment history and receipts.

## Controls

- Restrict sensitive reports and documents to authorized roles.
- Keep client records and uploaded files private.
- Enforce management-only authority for sensitive pricing and approval actions once confirmed by Elpis.
- Verify payment webhooks and reconcile provider transaction references before marking payments as successful.
- Do not store API secrets in source code or Git history.
